Thesis · University of York

Food systems, trade networks and adult obesity

MSc dissertation. Does the structure of a country's food-trade network help explain adult obesity beyond its domestic conditions? A panel of 4,574 rows covering 169 countries (2011–2024) from four public sources, with network features such as supplier concentration. Of 12 models benchmarked, a five-seed MLP ensemble cut MAE from 8.16 to 2.10 percentage points against a context-only model, and gave 32% lower MAE on unseen countries (5.81 vs 8.53) under nested country-blocked validation. Explained with SHAP.

Co-author. I implemented the code, co-developed the method and wrote the first draft. Under impulse noise on the wave equation, the ensemble cut MAE by 15% and prediction-interval width by about 80% against a standard PINN.

Project · Financial time series

Forecasting next-week min and max returns

Six temporal and graph models on 26 equities, with stocks as nodes and edges from return correlations, over 9 leakage-aware folds. CNN-LSTM won with a mean interval MAE of 0.0222, 12.8% below the Temporal GCN baseline.

Project · Cloud data engineering

London urban analytics lakehouse: testing the broken-windows theory

Bronze–Silver–Gold Delta Lake pipelines in PySpark, loading 7 public datasets through Azure Data Factory into a validated 416-row panel feeding four Tableau dashboards. A random forest on a time-based split raised test R² from 0.17 to 0.88. The fly-tipping effect fell from 0.305 to 0.210 after controls, so findings are framed as association.
