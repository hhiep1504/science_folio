Graph models for time series

Forecasting return intervals for a network of stocks

Can a graph over stocks help forecast the next week's minimum and maximum return? I compared six temporal and graph models on 26 equities, with stocks as nodes and edges from return correlations, over 9 leakage-aware folds. A CNN-LSTM won with a mean interval MAE of 0.0222, 12.8% below the Temporal GCN baseline, so in this setting the graph did not beat the simpler model.

Uncertainty quantification

Stochastic ensembles of physics-informed neural networks

Standard physics-informed networks give a single answer that degrades under noisy data. With colleagues at the HUST Computer Vision Lab I co-developed an ensemble that combines bagging with Monte Carlo dropout, and I implemented the code and wrote the first draft. Under impulse noise on the wave equation it cut MAE by 15% and prediction-interval width by about 80% against a standard PINN.

Explainable analysis on networks

Trade networks and adult obesity

Does the structure of a country's food-trade network explain adult obesity beyond its domestic conditions? Using a panel of 169 countries from 2011 to 2024, I added network features such as supplier concentration and benchmarked 12 models. A five-seed MLP ensemble cut MAE from 8.16 to 2.10 percentage points against a context-only model, and still gave 32% lower MAE on unseen countries under nested country-blocked validation. Models are explained with SHAP, and findings are framed as association, not cause.
